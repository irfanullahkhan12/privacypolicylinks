Privacy policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nedo Bird: Flappy Fly ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Last updated 14 July 2026

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. However, the third-party services below may automatically collect limited data:

  • Advertising identifiers & device information — collected by our ad provider (Google AdMob) to display and measure ads. This may include your device's advertising ID, IP-based approximate location (country/region), device model, and ad-interaction events.
  • Purchase information — when you make an in-app purchase (e.g. "Remove Ads" or premium birds), Google Play and our purchase provider (RevenueCat) process the transaction and record your purchase status so we can deliver what you bought and restore purchases.
  • Basic app usage & diagnostics — anonymous, aggregated data such as crashes or gameplay events may be used to keep the App working correctly.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display ads and keep the App free to play.
  • To process and restore your in-app purchases.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App's performance.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (in-app purchases) — https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data Security

Data transmitted by the App and its service providers is protected using industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) while in transit.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain purchase records only as long as needed to provide the service. Because the App has no user accounts, most data is controlled by the third-party services above. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. To request deletion of any data associated with you, contact us at the email below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
