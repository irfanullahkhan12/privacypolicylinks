Totomoto

Privacy policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Totomoto ("the App", "we", "us"), a 3D toad buddy app for children and families made by Bathan Dev, handles information when you use the App. The App is built so that it does not collect, store or share personal information.

Last updated 2 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

None. The App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, photos, contacts or device identifiers, and nothing you say or type is sent to us.

2. Permissions the App Uses

  • Microphone — so you can talk to Toto. See section 3.
  • Internet — only to download Toto's on-device AI model the first time it is needed. See section 5.

3. Microphone

The microphone is only used while you tap the mic button. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition service (for example, Google speech services on Android), which may process the audio under its own privacy policy. The App does not record, save or send your voice to us.

4. Chatting with Toto

Toto's answers come from built-in replies and an AI model that runs on your phone. Your messages are not sent to us or to any AI service, and the conversation is not saved after you close the App.

5. Internet Use

The first time it is needed, the App downloads its on-device AI model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any download, this shares the standard technical details of the request (such as your IP address) with that website. No personal information is sent.

6. Purchases

The App is a paid app sold through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your card or payment details. The App has no in-app purchases.

7. Third-Party Services

These services are involved in using the App, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play and Google speech services — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Hugging Face (AI model download) — https://huggingface.co/privacy

8. Children's Privacy

The App is designed to be safe for children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else. The "kids lock" feature only pins the App on screen; it does not collect any data. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at the email below.

9. Data Stored on Your Device

The downloaded AI model is saved in the App's private storage on your phone so Toto can chat without internet. It contains no personal information. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

10. Sharing and Selling of Data

We do not sell, rent or share any personal information with anyone, because the App does not collect any.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
